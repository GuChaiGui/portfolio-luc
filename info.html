Luc Ung is a Franco-Chinese cinematographer based in Los Angeles. Born and raised in La Rochelle, France, his love for cinema began at 16, through a chance encounter that led him to the La Rochelle International Film Festival.

A 2019 graduate of NYU Tisch School of the Arts, Luc spent several years in the lighting department on union film and television productions (IATSE Local 52) before moving into cinematography full-time — giving him a hands-on, technically grounded understanding of light that still shapes his work behind the camera.

Languages: English, French, Mandarin & Spanish
